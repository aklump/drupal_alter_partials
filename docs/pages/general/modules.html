Provide Partials by Module

  1. Your modules can also provide alter partials but they must declare themselves using _hook_alter_partialsinfo. See _alterpartials.api.php for documentation.
  2. Implement _hook_alter_partialsinfo in your module.
  3. Follow the theme instructions, except that you will create that files inside your module's _alterpartials directory.
